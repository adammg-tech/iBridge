# Contributing to iBridge

Thank you for your interest in contributing to iBridge!

## How to Contribute

1. Open an issue to discuss significant changes before starting work.
2. Fork the repository and make your changes on a separate branch.
3. Test your changes and submit a pull request describing what you changed.

All contributions are subject to review and may be accepted or rejected by the project maintainer.
