# iBridge Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 15, 2026

**Developer:** Adam MG Alsherif  
**Application:** iBridge  
**Package Name:** com.adammg.ibridge

## 1. Introduction

iBridge is an open-source Android application designed to connect Android devices with Apple iPhones through a physical USB connection.

Your privacy is important. iBridge is designed to operate locally, without collecting or transmitting your personal information to the developer or external servers.

## 2. Information Collection

iBridge does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information to the developer or third parties.

The application does not include:
- Advertising or advertising identifiers.
- Analytics or user-tracking systems.
- User accounts or registration.
- Remote telemetry or crash-reporting services.
- Remote databases or cloud synchronization.

## 3. Device Permissions

iBridge requests certain Android permissions to provide its functionality:

- **Foreground Service:** Maintains the USB communication service while connected.
- **Foreground Service – Connected Device:** Supports the connected-device foreground service.
- **Notifications:** Displays the USB service notification.
- **Storage Permissions:** Allows access to audio files and the saving or exporting of files, photos, and backups.
- **Internet:** Supports local loopback socket communication required by the USB bridge and opening user-requested external links.

The application also requires USB Host hardware support to communicate with connected iPhones.

## 4. USB Communication

iBridge communicates directly with connected iPhones through a physical USB connection.

USB communication and device discovery are handled locally using bundled open-source components and Apple device communication protocols.

The application does not send connected-device information to remote servers.

## 5. Files, Photos, Backups, and Ringtones

Depending on the features you use, iBridge may access:

- Files stored on your connected iPhone.
- Photos and videos in the iPhone DCIM directory.
- Device backups created through the application.
- Audio files selected for ringtone conversion.
- Ringtone files transferred to your iPhone.

These operations are performed locally between your Android device and connected iPhone.

Exported files and backups are stored on your Android device, either in application storage or in the destination directory selected for export.

## 6. Logs and Diagnostic Information

iBridge provides access to iOS system logs and crash reports through its USB connection.

These diagnostic records may contain device identifiers, device information, timestamps, or other information generated by iOS.

Logs and crash reports are retrieved and displayed locally. They are not automatically uploaded or transmitted to the developer.

Users should treat diagnostic records as potentially sensitive information.

## 7. Data Storage and Retention

Application data, temporary files, ringtone backups, and diagnostic information are stored locally on your Android device.

iBridge does not maintain remote servers or cloud storage for user information.

You can remove exported files through your device's file manager. Application-private data is generally removed when the application is uninstalled.

## 8. Third-Party Services and Libraries

iBridge uses open-source software components and Android Jetpack libraries to provide its functionality.

The application does not integrate advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services.

Opening external links, such as the GitHub repository, is initiated by the user and handled by the relevant external application or browser.

## 9. Children's Privacy

iBridge does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. No user registration or personal information submission is required.

## 10. Security

iBridge is designed to keep file operations and device communication local.

Users should protect their Android devices, connected iPhones, exported files, and backups against unauthorized access.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or data-handling practices change.

Any updated version will be published at the same Privacy Policy URL.
